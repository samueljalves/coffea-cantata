Coffee origins

The legend of Kaldi.

According to an Ethiopian legend, a goat herder named Kaldi noticed his goats becoming unusually energetic after eating red coffee cherries. In a popular telling, he tried the cherries himself and experienced the same effect.

The story continues at a monastery, where a drink made from the cherries helped the monks stay awake. It remains one of coffee’s best-known origin legends.
