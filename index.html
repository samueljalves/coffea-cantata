Coffee origins

The Legend of Kaldi
and the Dancing Goats.

According to an Ethiopian legend, a goat herder named Kaldi noticed his goats becoming unusually energetic after eating red coffee cherries. In a popular telling, he tried the cherries himself and experienced the same effect.

The story continues at a monastery, where a drink made from the cherries helped the monks stay awake. It remains one of coffee’s best-known origin legends.

Coffee in India

How coffee came to India.

Indian coffee tradition tells of Baba Budan, a Sufi pilgrim who is said to have brought seven unroasted coffee seeds from Mocha in Yemen to the hills near Chikkamagaluru, Karnataka, after a pilgrimage to Mecca in the seventeenth century.

According to the story, he planted the seeds in the hills now known as Baba Budangiri. Those seven seeds are remembered as an important beginning in India’s coffee-growing history.
