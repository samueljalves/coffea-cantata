01 · Origins

From a cherry to a cantata.

Kaldi’s dancing goats

In an Ethiopian legend, a herder named Kaldi noticed his goats leaping with unusual energy after eating red coffee cherries. Curiosity got the better of him, and he tried the fruit himself.

Across ports and hills

From Ethiopia, coffee travelled through Yemen and the Ottoman world into Europe. Indian tradition tells of Baba Budan carrying seven seeds from Yemen to the hills of Karnataka.

Coffee finds its voice

In Leipzig, Johann Sebastian Bach turned a young woman’s devotion to her daily coffee into a musical comedy. His Coffee Cantata gives our name its rhythm and its sense of play.
